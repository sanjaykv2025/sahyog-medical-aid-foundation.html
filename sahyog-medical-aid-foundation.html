Mission Statement

Our Mission

To support financially vulnerable families facing medical emergencies by helping them access verified treatment assistance, essential medicines, and emergency healthcare support.

We are committed to dignity, transparency, responsible use of donations, and regular reporting of the assistance provided to beneficiaries.

Need Help?

Facing a medical emergency?

Tell us about the patient's situation. Our team will contact you to understand the need and guide you on the next steps.

Please keep these ready:
  • Patient's ID proof (Aadhaar or other)
  • Doctor's prescription / medical reports
  • Hospital estimate or bills
  • Family income proof (if available)

Details are sent via WhatsApp to our team. Please do not share bank PIN/OTP with anyone.

FAQ

Common questions

Who can receive assistance?

Financially vulnerable families facing a medical emergency. Every request is verified before support is arranged.

How do I know my donation is used properly?

We are committed to responsible use of donations and regular reporting of assistance provided. Check the Transparency section and ask us for updates anytime.

Will I get a receipt?

Share your payment details with us on WhatsApp or email and we will send an acknowledgement. Ask us about tax-benefit eligibility for your donation.

Is there any fee to apply for help?

No. Never pay anyone for assistance from Sahyog. Contact us only through the official details on this website.
